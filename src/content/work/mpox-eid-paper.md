Continuing my work with WHO, I served as co-first author on a publication in the U.S. CDC's *Emerging Infectious Diseases* journal examining mpox cases among children and adolescents during the 2022–23 outbreak. While the outbreak predominantly affected adult men, 1.3% of reported cases were in people under 18. Analysis of global surveillance data showed one ICU admission and zero deaths in that age group, with transmission routes and clinical presentation varying across age subgroups.
