As a part-time student researcher with the Applied Sustainability Science group at the [Mercator Research Institute on Global Commons and Climate Change (MCC)](https://www.mcc-berlin.net/), I built a living map of research on climate change and human health. I trained supervised and unsupervised machine learning models in Python to classify scientific literature, and ran citation network analysis to show how publications in the field connect.
