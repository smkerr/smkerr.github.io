With D. Saulo I co-wrote an article for the IOA _Field Exchange_ ([volume 9](https://reliefweb.int/report/world/ioa-field-exchange-volume-9-january-2025), January 2025) about the mpox analytics community of practice run by the WHO Hub. From September 2024 to October 2025 I co-organized and moderated 14 calls where modelers and analysts from public health agencies and academia presented their latest research on the outbreak. I selected topics, invited speakers, drafted agendas, shared resources with members and ran bilingual English-French sessions.

I presented the community at a joint meeting of WHO, Africa CDC, the Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations (CEPI) and the US National Institutes of Health on the mpox research agenda, and at a dengue analytics workshop, where it became the model for a new dengue community.
