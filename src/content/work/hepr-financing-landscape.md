I built an R data pipeline for a WHO landscape analysis of financing for health emergency preparedness and response (HEPR). It estimates development finance across five core components, combining data from the OECD Creditor Reporting System (CRS), the Global Fund, the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME) and multilateral development banks, with near-term projections to 2028. I delivered slide decks and a methodology note, plus documented procedures so that the analysis can be repeated by others.
