With classmates Kai Foerster, Danial Riaz, and Lukas Lehmann at the Hertie School, I examined the network dynamics within EU public procurement markets using a dataset of 234 networks across 26 European countries from 2008 to 2016.

The networks comprised issuers, or "buyers" such as public hospitals and ministries, and winners, or "suppliers" of public contracts, mainly private-sector firms. We explored network characteristics related to different levels of perceived government corruption. Slovakia's 2014 public procurement market, for example, showed several clusters of high single-bidding rates, a metric linked to corruption risk.
