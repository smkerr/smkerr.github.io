Following Hamas's October 7 attack on Israel and the subsequent Israeli military campaign in Gaza, media coverage of the events quickly became polarized. With classmates Kai Foerster, Danial Riaz, and Max Eckert, I investigated how news coverage evolved over time and how it varied by outlet.

We sourced over 4,000 articles from outlets including The New York Times, Al Jazeera, and Die Welt, published in the weeks immediately preceding and following the October 7 attack, and used topic modeling to identify recurring themes and sentiment analysis to measure how emotional tone varied by outlet.
