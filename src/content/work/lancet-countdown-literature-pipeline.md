As a student worker at the [Mercator Research Institute on Global Commons and Climate Change (MCC)](https://www.mcc-berlin.net/) in 2024, I worked on the literature pipeline behind the Lancet Countdown indicator on scientific articles on health and climate change. The pipeline finds new research, classifies it with machine learning, and extracts where each study took place.

I reorganized the code and streamlined the update process.
