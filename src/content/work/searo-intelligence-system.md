I led requirements and technical design for a new system that turns public health signals from the Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources (EIOS) platform into daily briefing emails, weekly situation updates and epidemiological bulletins. It is now in use at the WHO Regional Office for South-East Asia (SEARO).

I acted as the link between SEARO and the development team, interviewing staff about their needs, translating them into technical specifications, and presenting the design and progress to the client. I also prototyped the system's AI features and handed them to the development team to build into the platform and refine, and made sure the build met WHO's cybersecurity and development guidelines.
