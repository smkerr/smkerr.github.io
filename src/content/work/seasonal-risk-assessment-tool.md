I led development of the WHO Seasonal Risk Assessment Tool, published by WHO in November 2026. It turns the method behind the winter and summer risk assessments of the WHO Country Office in Ukraine into a generic tool that other emergency settings can reuse. I first worked on that method in 2022-23, when I supported the Ukraine country office's [winter risk assessment](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/m/item/risk-assessment-for-winter-in-ukraine-december-2022): I identified data sources and helped develop a structured way to score seasonal risk.

For the tool, I created the Excel workbook and the R Shiny app, wrote the user documentation, and released the source code. I coordinated the WHO Hub and the WHO Country Office in Ukraine, presented the tool to internal WHO teams, and managed WHO's publication clearance process. A manuscript describing the methodology and tool is currently in submission.
