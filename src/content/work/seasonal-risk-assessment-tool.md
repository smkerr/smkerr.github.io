I led development of the WHO Seasonal Risk Assessment Tool, published by WHO in November 2026. It scores winter and summer risks to people in emergency settings across geographic regions within a country, helping decision-makers prioritize where to direct resources. Built on the method behind the risk assessments of the WHO Country Office in Ukraine, it is flexible and reusable, so other emergency settings can apply it to their own context. I first worked on that method in 2022-23, when I supported the Ukraine country office's [winter risk assessment](https://www.who.int/europe/publications/m/item/risk-assessment-for-winter-in-ukraine-december-2022) by identifying data sources and helping develop a structured way to score seasonal risk.

For the tool, I created the Excel workbook and the R Shiny app, wrote the user documentation, and released the source code. I coordinated the WHO Hub and the WHO Country Office in Ukraine and presented the tool to internal WHO teams. A manuscript describing the methodology and tool is currently in submission.
