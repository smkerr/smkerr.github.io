[grEPI](https://collaboratory.who.int/epidemiologicalparameters/repository/) is a repository of epidemiological parameters such as incubation periods and serial intervals, run by the WHO Hub. In June 2025 I led a session for the WHO Collaboratory's Epidemiological Parameters community of practice showing how grEPI can summarize the parameters for a disease, using mpox as the example and drawing on the switch of the WHO mpox dashboard's parameter section to grEPI. Since December 2025 I have served as a subject matter expert on the core development team, and a proof of concept launched in April 2026.

On the team I advise on parameter data structure, API access and downstream use in R packages and AI pipelines, and I draft user guidance. I keep grEPI data usable in R by contributing [fixes](https://github.com/epiverse-trace/epiparameter/pulls?q=is:pr+author:smkerr) to the [{epiparameter}](https://github.com/epiverse-trace/epiparameter) package, maintained by the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (LSHTM). I also reviewed comparable repositories to inform the governance model and proposed designs for community feedback, versioning, parameter lifecycle tracking, API features and unique identifiers. I co-moderated community consultations, helped plan sessions of the core technical working group (LSHTM, the Public Health Agency of Canada and Imperial College London), and presented grEPI in several external forums.
